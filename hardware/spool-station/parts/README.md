# Bought parts

Models of the bought parts that each roller uses, in the assembly and the exploded view.

| File | Part | By | License |
|---|---|---|---|
| `M5_ISO7380_button.step` | M5 button head socket screw, ISO 7380, 15 mm; the build lengthens its shank to 20 mm | k2-mirror, from the [FreeCAD parts library](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD-library) | [CC-BY 3.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) |
| `M5_ISO7092_washer.step` | M5 flat washer, ISO 7092 (DIN 433), 9 mm across | nachotineo, from the [FreeCAD parts library](https://github.com/FreeCAD/FreeCAD-library) | [CC-BY 3.0](http://creativecommons.org/licenses/by/3.0/) |

The 605ZZ bearing, 5 x 14 x 5 mm, is built by `build_spool_station.py` from its standard
dimensions: no openly licensed model of it turned up. A model saved here as `605ZZ.step`,
axis along x and centred, is used instead.
